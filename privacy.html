Nova Cinematic — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2025

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Nova Cinematic (“we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, and shares personal information when you interact with our TikTok automation tools and related services (“Service”).

2. Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Account details provided through TikTok OAuth (such as your TikTok user ID and display name).
  • Content and prompts you submit for rendering.
  • Usage logs, diagnostics, and feedback you provide to support teams.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to operate, maintain, and improve the Service; fulfill content rendering and publishing requests; communicate with you about updates; and meet legal or regulatory obligations.

4. Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers who support the Service (such as cloud infrastructure or content storage) and as required by law or to protect our rights.

5. Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to deliver the Service or as required by law. You may request deletion of stored data by contacting us.

6. Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information, including encryption in transit, access controls, and audit logging.

7. Your Choices

You can revoke TikTok access at any time via TikTok settings. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your information.

8. International Transfers

If we transfer personal information internationally, we implement safeguards consistent with applicable laws.

9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.

11. Contact

Questions or requests? Email support@novacinematic.com.
